Connecting reported cases to a simple SIR simulation

The team cleaned India’s Our World in Data case records, summarized weekly reporting, estimated early growth parameters and compared three transmission scenarios using a Susceptible–Infected–Recovered model.

The analysis covered 1,682 India records from 5 Jan 2020 to 12 Aug 2024. Python, Pandas, NumPy and Matplotlib supported the data work and visualizations.
